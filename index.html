① Hadiah (Tawasul)

1. ILA HADROTIN NABIYYIL MUSTHOFA MUHAMMADIN SHALLALLAHU 'ALAIHI WASALLAM, WA 'ALA ALIHI WA AZWAJIHI WA DZURRIYYATIHI WA AHLI BAITIHI KIROMI, SYAI’UN LILLAHI LAHUMUL FAATIHAH...

2. WA ILA ARWAHI ABA-IHI WA IKHWANIHI MINAL AMBIYA-I WAL MURSALIN, WA ALI KULLIN MINHUM WASH-SHOHABATI AJMA'IN, KHUSHUSHON AL-KHULAFA-IR ROSYIDIN, SADATINA ABI BAKRIN WA 'UMARO WA 'UTSMANA WA 'ALIYYIN RODIALLAHU 'ANHUM, SYAI’UN LILLAHI LAHUMUL FAATIHAH...

3. TSUMMA ILA ARWAHI SYAIKHINA SYAIKH MUHYIDDIN ABDUL QODIR AL-JAILANI, SHOHIBIL BAGHDAD, QODDASALLAHU SIRROHUN NURONI, SYAI’UN LILLAHI LAHUMUL FAATIHAH...

BISMILLAH...
ALIF LAAM MIIM. DZALIKAL KITABU LAA ROIBA FIIHI HUDAL LIL MUTTAQIIN. ALLADZIINA YU'MINUUNA BIL GHOIBI WA YUQIIMUUNASH SHOLAATA WA MIMMAA ROZAQNAAHUM YUNFIQUUN. WALLADZIINA YU'MINUUNA BIMAA UNZILA ILAIKA WA MAA UNZILA MIN QOBLIK, WA BIL AKHIROTI HUM YUUQINUUN. ULAA-IKA 'ALAA HUDAM MIR ROBBIHIM WA ULAA-IKA HUMUL MUFLIHUUN.
WA ILAAHUKUM ILAAHUW WAAHIDUN LAA ILAAHA ILLAA HUWAR ROHMAANUR ROHIIM.

ALLAAHU LAA ILAAHA ILLAA HUWAL HAYYUL QOYYUUM, LAA TA’KHUDZUHUU SINATUW WALAA NAUUM, LAHUU MAA FIS SAMAAWAATI WA MAA FIL ARDH, MAN DZALLADZII YASYFA’U ‘INDAHUU ILLAA BI IDZNIH, YA’LAMU MAA BAINA AIDIIHIM WA MAA KHOLFAHUM, WALAA YUHIITHUUNA BI SYAI-IM MIN ‘ILMIHII ILLAA BIMAA SYAA-A, WASI’A KURSIYYUHUS SAMAAWAATI WAL ARDH, WALAA YA-UUDUHUU HIFZHUHUMAA WAHUWAL ‘ALIYYUL ‘ADZHIIM.

ALHAMDULILLAHI RABBIL 'ALAMIN

MUDAH MUDAHAN NGAOSAN HADIAH SARENG YASIN IYEU DI TAMPI KU ALLAH SWT.
SARENG MUDAH MUDAHAN ALMARHUM SARENG ALMARHUMAH KHUSUNA ALMARHUM BAPAK EMAD BIN ATA
UMUMNA KA KAUM MUSLIMIN WAL MUSLIMAT WAL MUKMININ WALMUKMINAT.

MUDAH MUDAHAN ALMARHUM DI TAMPI IMAN ISLAMNA, DIHAPUSKEN SAGALA DOSA-DOSANA, DIHAPUNTEN TINA SAGALA KALEPATAN SARENG KHILAFNA, DILIPAT GANDAKEUN PAHALANA, DI CAANGKEN SARENG DI LEGA KEUN DI ALAM KUBURNA, SARENG MUDAH MUDAHAN DI TEMPATKEN DI SYURGA NA ALLAH NU PANGSAENA.

ATUH URANG NU NGAOSANA MUDAH-MUDAHAN JADI AMAL KASAEAN, NAON-NAON NU DI PIKAHAYANG MUDAH-MUDAHAN TIASA KA KOBUL SARENG MUDAH-MUDAHAN DIPASIHAN KABERKAHAN, KASALAMETAN SARENG DI MUDAHKEUN KANA REZEKINA NU BAROKAH, AMIN YA ROBBAL ALAMIN.
